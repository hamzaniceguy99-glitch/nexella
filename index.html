How we work

No affiliate links, no wiring

Nexella takes no commission from any brand. Half of good smart home advice is telling people not to buy something, and that is impossible when the advice is paid for by a catalog.

There is also work we won't touch: anything behind the drywall. Smart switches that replace mains wiring, new circuits and panel work go to a licensed electrician, and we are not an alarm monitoring company — no device here is a substitute for one.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Will you tell me which brand to buy?

Yes, with reasons, and we earn nothing either way. Sometimes the answer is to keep what you own.

Can you help me install a smart switch?

We can explain what it needs and how it fits your system. The wiring itself is for a licensed electrician — mains work is not a video-call job.

I rent. Is this pointless?

Not at all. Plugs, bulbs, sensors and a decent network do most of the useful work and come with you when you move.

Is a smart home a security system?

No. Cameras and sensors are useful, but this is not monitored security and we won't pretend a doorbell camera prevents anything.

What about privacy?

It is a whole program. We go through what each device collects, what stays local, and the state rules about recording audio and neighbors.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.
