How we work

One platform, few devices, real automations

Buying a plug here and a bulb there produces three apps, two hubs and nothing that runs by itself. Choosing a platform first is unglamorous and it is the whole game.

Then automations have to survive real life. If the lights fail when a guest's phone is on the wifi, or nobody but you can turn the heat up, the system gets switched off within a month.

And most 'faulty device' complaints are network complaints. Fixing coverage and channels quietly resolves a long list of problems people were about to spend money on.
