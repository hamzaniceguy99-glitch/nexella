The programs, in detail

Four programs of 6 to 10 weeks, worked on the devices and network you already have.

Program 01 · Commit to one

Pick a platform

Most frustration comes from three half-used apps. We work out which platform fits your phones, your household and your tolerance for tinkering, then plan a first set of devices with a naming scheme you won't hate in a year.

  • Beginners
  • 2 hours a week
  • A phone and wifi

Program 02 · Nobody opens the app

Automations that stick

A smart home you have to operate is worse than switches. We build automations around presence, motion, time and door sensors, then test the failure modes: what happens when the internet drops, the phone dies, or a guest stays over.

  • 8 weeks
  • Have some devices
  • 3 hours a week
  • Your existing kit

Program 03 · The real problem

Network & wifi

Devices that drop off are usually a coverage or channel problem, not a bad device. We map your home, fix placement and channels, decide whether mesh is warranted, and put smart devices on a network that keeps them away from your laptop.

  • 6 weeks
  • Any level
  • 2 hours a week
  • Router access

Weeks 1–2 · Survey

Weeks 3–4 · Fixes

Weeks 5–6 · Segmentation

A separate network for devices, done simply.

Weeks 1–2 · Inventory

What you own, what it collects, where it sends it.

Weeks 3–4 · Choices

Local storage, cloud plans and what each costs you.

Weeks 5–6 · Boundaries

Placement, audio and household agreement.

Program 04 · Decided, not drifted into

Cameras & privacy

Cameras and microphones are the part people install first and think about last. We go through cloud versus local storage, subscriptions, sharing with family, and the laws about recording audio and pointing cameras where other people live — which vary by state and are your responsibility.

  • 6 weeks
  • Any level
  • 2 hours a week
